# 🌟 Key Features

## 🎙️ Voice Points System

- Earn points while spending time in voice channels.
- Check your balance or another player's balance with `/points`.
- Send points to another player with `/givepoints`; enter a number, percentage, or `all`.

## 🏙️ City Builder

- Browse buildings and their prices and income with `/buildings`.
- Buy a building with `/buybuilding`, choosing a building and an empty `(x, y)` location on your city grid.
- View your city or another player's city with `/city`.
- Rename your city with `/setcityname`.
- Move a building between grid coordinates with `/movebuilding`, or sell it from its current `(x, y)` location with `/sellbuilding`.
- Collect the income generated by your buildings with `/collectpoints`.

## 🎣 Fishing System

- Browse available fish and their weight ranges with `/fish`.
- Start fishing with `/fishing` for 10 points; when a fish bites, react with 🎣 before time runs out to reel it in.
- Sell fish with `/sellfish`; choose a fish and sell one by weight, the heaviest, the lightest, or all matching fish.
- Configure the server's fish list with `/editfish`.

## 🎮 Pokémon System

- Buy Poké Balls in `/shop`: Poké Ball (1,000 points, ×1), Great Ball (5,000, ×1.5), Ultra Ball (10,000, ×2), or Master Ball (20,000, ×5).
- Meet a random Pokémon with `/pokemonencounter`; choose a ball by reacting or flee with 🏃 without spending one.
- Browse Pokémon sprites in a 3×3 grid with `/pokedex`; names, Pokédex numbers, and duplicate counts appear together in the list below the grid. Use the ⬅️ Previous and Next ➡️ buttons to change pages; they appear when the collection has multiple pages.
- View a Pokémon's details with `/pokemoninfo`.
- Trade a duplicate Pokémon for a standard Poké Ball with `/pokemontrade`.
- Give items or individual Pokémon to another player with `/giveitem`.

## 🎰 Casino

- Bet a number of points, a percentage of your balance, or `all` with `/gamble`; the coin-flip result either wins or loses the bet.
- Play `/cards` against the bot: the higher card wins the wager.
- Play `/slots` for a 10-point bet; matching symbols across a row, column, or diagonal wins the shared pool.
- Check the current raffle pool with `/checkraffle` and your tickets with `/checktickets`.
- Buy raffle tickets with `/buyticket`; the first ticket costs 100 points, and each additional ticket costs 50 points more than the previous one.
- Challenge another player to `/duel` for a point wager; they can accept or decline, and the winner is chosen at random.

## 🛍️ Shop and Inventory

- Browse server shop items and your next prices with `/shop`.
- Buy an item with `/buy`; repeat purchases of regular items increase their price.
- View your items and fish with `/items`; view Pokémon separately with `/pokedex`.
- Create or remove server shop items with `/createShopItem` and `/removeShopItem`.
- Send items or an individual Pokémon to another player with `/giveitem`.

## 🎮 Games

- Start a five-letter Wordle game with `/wordle`; guess with `/wguess` in up to six attempts before the five-minute timer expires.
- Start Hangman with `/hangman`; use `/hguess` to guess a letter or the whole word before you run out of attempts.
- Generate a random number from 1 through your chosen maximum with `/random`.

## 🏆 Highscores and Statistics

- Check your points or another player's balance with `/points`.
- View the top 10 players in a category with `/highscore`.
- Review your game, fishing, city, and point-transfer statistics with `/stats`.

## 🎵 YouTube Music Player

- Use `/play` with a YouTube song, search query, or playlist to start playback or add it to the queue.
- Add a track to the front of the queue with `/next`, or interrupt playback with `/playnow` and a single track.
- Control playback with `/pause`, `/resume`, `/skip`, and `/stop`.
- See the currently playing song with `/nowplaying`, its position with `/position`, and the upcoming queue with `/queue`.
- Set volume from 0% to 1,000% with `/volume`; `/shuffle` randomly reorders the queue, and `/queue` shows the resulting playback order.
- Join a voice channel before using music commands.

## 🌐 Information Search

- Find an image matching a query with `/image`.
- Ask a question with `/chatgpt`.
- Get a random Reddit meme with `/meme` or a random Wikipedia article with `/wiki`.
- Check a city's current weather with `/weather` or its forecast with `/forecast`.

## 🛠️ Management Commands

- Browse command help by category with `/help`.
- Administrators can set the default member role with `/defaultRole` and configure image-only channels with `/imageOnly`.
- Configure the raffle channel with `/raffleChannel` and the message-deletion reaction with `/deleteMessageEmoji`.
- Manage the fish list and shop items with `/editfish`, `/createShopItem`, and `/removeShopItem`.
- Administrators can update Lavalink settings with `/lavalink` and restart the bot with `/restart`.
